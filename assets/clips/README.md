# Your soccer clips go here

Drop video files (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`, …) into this folder and the agent will
cut them into your Shorts, with the AI voiceover and captions on top.

**You are responsible for the rights to anything you place here.** Use:
- footage you filmed yourself,
- clips you've licensed (stock, or directly from the rights holder),
- or genuinely rights-cleared / Creative Commons material.

Do **not** drop in clips ripped from YouTube/TikTok/broadcasts you don't have
rights to — YouTube's Content ID will flag them and can take your channel down.

## Optional: per-topic clips
Create a subfolder whose name contains the topic slug to scope clips to one video,
e.g. `assets/clips/world-cup-dark-horses/`. Otherwise every clip here is fair game
for any video.
